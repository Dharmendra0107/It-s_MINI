04 // MINI
Your Personal AI Operating System

Meet MINI

Multifunctional Intelligent Neural Interface

MINI is a local-first personal AI assistant built for productivity, automation, and developer workflow support. It combines voice interaction, AI decision support, system automation, project intelligence, and task management into one unified desktop experience — helping you work faster, automate repetitive actions, and keep tasks, notes, and history organized without switching between a dozen disconnected apps.

Think Less. Automate More.

CALM INTELLIGENT RELIABLE ADAPTIVE LOCAL-FIRST
Vision

An AI assistant that feels active and integrated into daily work — not limited to chat, and not dependent on the cloud to be useful.

Mission

Consolidate personal assistance, desktop automation, project support, and developer tools into one practical, local-first platform.
